We ship to Canada, the United Kingdom, the European Union, Australia and New Zealand with DHL Express.

## Delivery times and prices

International orders arrive in 4–8 business days after they ship. Shipping costs $24.95, or is free on orders over $200. Fuel canisters, lithium batteries sold on their own and some knives can't be shipped internationally; the product page says so.

## Duties and taxes

Prices on our site don't include import duties, taxes or customs fees. These are set by the destination country and are paid by the recipient, usually to DHL before delivery. DHL contacts you by email or text with the amount and a payment link. If they aren't paid, the package is held at customs and returned to us after about 2 weeks, and we refund the order minus the shipping cost.

## Customs delays

Customs can hold a package for inspection for a few days. DHL updates the tracking page when it's released. If a package has been held for more than 5 business days without a request for payment, contact us and we'll ask DHL for details.

## Returns from outside the United States

International returns aren't prepaid. Ship the items back with a tracked service and send us the tracking number; we refund the items once they arrive. Duties and taxes paid on import can usually be reclaimed from your customs authority, not from us.
