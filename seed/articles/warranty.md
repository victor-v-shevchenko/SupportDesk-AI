title: Kestrel Outfitters warranty
---

Every Kestrel-brand product is covered by our warranty against defects in materials and workmanship. Products from other brands are covered by their makers' warranties, and we help you file the claim.

## What's covered

Kestrel-brand gear is covered for 2 years from delivery; Kestrel electronics, such as the Trailhead GPS watch and Lumen headlamps, for 1 year. The warranty covers failed seams, broken zippers, delaminating fabric, faulty buckles and electronics that stop working under normal use.

## What isn't covered

Normal wear and tear, damage from accidents, misuse or improper care, alterations, and damage from animals or fire aren't covered. Neither is gear used commercially, such as rental fleets.

## Safety issues

If a product could be unsafe, such as a stove or fuel connector that leaks, a cracked carabiner or a frayed harness, stop using it right away. For a leaking stove, disconnect the fuel canister outdoors, away from any flame, and don't reattach it. Contact us and mark your message as a safety issue: safety claims are handled first, and we replace or refund the product without asking you to send it back first.

## How to make a claim

Contact support with your order number, a description of the problem and photos. We usually decide within 3 business days. If the claim is approved, we repair the product, replace it, or give you store credit if it's no longer made.
