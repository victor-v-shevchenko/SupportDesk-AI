title: Changing or cancelling an order
---

You can change or cancel an order while it's still being processed. Once it has shipped, changes are limited to what the carrier allows.

## While the order is processing

Orders stay in "processing" for about an hour before they're picked, and longer at busy times. During that time you can cancel the order yourself under **Account → Orders → Cancel order**. To change a size, color or item, contact us: we cancel the old item and add the new one, and any price difference is charged or refunded.

## After the order has shipped

We can't cancel or change a shipped order. You can refuse the delivery or return the items when they arrive; see the returns policy.

## Changing the delivery address

Before the order ships, we can change the address for you. After it ships, you can ask the carrier to redirect it to another address or hold it at a pickup point, using the carrier's own tools from the tracking link. Redirects aren't always possible and some carriers charge for them. We can't redirect international shipments.
