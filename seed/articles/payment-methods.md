You can pay with most cards and several digital wallets. Every payment is processed securely; we never see or store your full card number.

## Accepted methods

- Visa, Mastercard, American Express and Discover
- PayPal
- Apple Pay and Google Pay, on supported devices and browsers
- Klarna, to pay in 4 interest-free installments on orders from $50 to $1,000 (United States only)
- Kestrel gift cards, on their own or together with another method

## When you're charged

Your payment method is authorized when you place the order and charged when the order ships. If an order ships in more than one package, you're charged for each package as it ships. Klarna takes the first installment when the order ships.

## Payment problems at checkout

If a payment fails, check that the billing address matches the one your bank has, and that your card allows online purchases. For PayPal, make sure pop-ups are allowed and your PayPal account has a confirmed funding source. Browser extensions that block scripts or ads can also stop PayPal and Klarna from loading. If the problem continues with more than one method or browser, contact us with the error message and the time it happened, so we can check our payment logs.
