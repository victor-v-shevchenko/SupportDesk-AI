This article explains how long refunds take and where the money goes. For what can be returned, see the returns policy.

## How refunds are paid

Refunds always go back to the original payment method. Orders paid partly with a gift card are refunded to the gift card first, up to the amount taken from it, and the rest to the card or account you paid with.

## How long refunds take

| Situation | When we issue the refund |
| --- | --- |
| Return received at our warehouse | Within 2 business days after inspection |
| Order cancelled before it shipped | Same day |
| Lost package | When the carrier confirms the loss, usually within 5 business days |

After we issue a refund, your bank or card provider usually takes 5–10 business days to show it. PayPal refunds usually appear within 1 business day.

## Cancelled orders and pending charges

When you place an order, your card is authorized for the full amount; you're charged when the order ships. If you cancel before it ships, we release the authorization the same day, but some banks keep showing it as "pending" for up to 10 business days. If a charge from a cancelled order is still showing after 10 business days, contact us with your order number and we'll send you a confirmation you can show your bank.

## Duplicate charges

If you see two charges for one order, one of them is usually an authorization that will drop off on its own within a few days. If both charges have posted, contact us and we'll refund the duplicate within 2 business days.
