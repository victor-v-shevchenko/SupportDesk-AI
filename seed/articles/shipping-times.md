Kestrel Outfitters ships from our warehouse in Boise, Idaho. Orders placed before 1 pm Mountain Time on a business day usually leave the warehouse the same day.

## Delivery options in the United States

| Option | Delivery time | Price |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Standard | 3–5 business days | Free on orders over $75, otherwise $6.95 |
| Expedited | 2 business days | $14.95 |
| Overnight | Next business day | $29.95 |

Delivery times start when the order ships, not when it's placed. Alaska, Hawaii and US territories add 2–4 business days to every option.

## Tracking your order

When your order ships, we email you a tracking link from the carrier (UPS, USPS or FedEx). Tracking can take up to 24 hours to show the first scan. You can also see the status of every order under **Account → Orders**.

## Delayed shipments

Carriers sometimes hold packages because of weather, high volume or an address problem. If tracking shows "delayed", the carrier usually updates the delivery date within two business days. If there's no update after three business days, or your order hasn't arrived 10 business days after it shipped, contact us and we'll open a trace with the carrier.

## Lost packages

If a package hasn't moved for 7 business days, or tracking shows it was delivered but you can't find it, we treat it as lost. After checking with the carrier, we send a replacement at no cost or refund the order, whichever you prefer. Orders that have been in transit for more than 14 days are handled as a priority.
