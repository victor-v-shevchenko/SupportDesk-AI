We want you to love your gear. If something isn't right, you can return it for a refund or an exchange.

## Return window

Full-price items can be returned within 60 days of delivery. Items bought on sale or in the end-of-season clearance can be returned within 30 days of delivery. Final-sale items, marked "final sale" on the product page, can't be returned unless they arrived damaged or faulty.

## What can be returned

Items must be unused, clean and in their original packaging with tags attached. You're welcome to try clothing and footwear on indoors to check the fit; boots worn only indoors on carpet still count as unused. We can't accept items that show signs of outdoor use, such as dirt on soles, campfire smell, sap or worn-in straps, unless you're making a warranty claim, which follows the warranty process instead of this one. Personalized items, opened fuel canisters, opened food, and hygiene items such as water bladders and mouthpieces that have been unsealed can't be returned for safety reasons. Gift cards can't be returned. Bundles and kits have to be returned complete: if you return only part of a kit, we refund the price of that part as listed separately on the site at the time of purchase, which can be less than its share of the kit price. If you received a free gift with a promotion and return the item that qualified for it, please send the gift back too, or we deduct its listed value from your refund. Returns that arrive without the original packaging, or with missing parts, may be refunded at a reduced amount after inspection; we email you before doing this, and you can choose to have the item sent back to you instead at no cost.

## How to start a return

1. Go to **Account → Orders**, choose the order and select **Return items**
2. Pick the items and a reason, and choose a refund or an exchange
3. Print the prepaid return label and drop the package at any UPS location

Return shipping is free within the United States. If you don't have an account, use the guest order lookup with your order number and email address.

## Exchanges

For a different size or color of the same item, choose **Exchange** when you start the return. We ship the new item as soon as the carrier scans your return, so you don't have to wait for it to reach us. If the size you want is out of stock, we refund you instead.
