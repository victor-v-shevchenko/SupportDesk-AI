Kestrel clothing uses US sizes. Each product page has its own size chart; this guide explains how to measure and how our fits compare.

## Measuring yourself

Measure over light clothing. Chest: around the fullest part, under the arms. Waist: around your natural waist. Inseam: from the crotch to the floor, without shoes. If you're between sizes, choose the larger size for layering pieces such as shells and insulated jackets.

## Fits

- **Trim fit:** close to the body, for base layers and running wear
- **Regular fit:** room for one mid layer underneath
- **Relaxed fit:** room for a fleece or light insulated layer

## Footwear

Kestrel boots run true to size for most people. Hiking boots should leave about a thumb's width between your longest toe and the end of the boot, with the socks you'll hike in. Try boots on in the afternoon, when your feet are a little larger. Half sizes are available from 7 to 12.

## Tent footprints

Each Ridgeline footprint is cut to fit one tent size: the Ridgeline 2 footprint fits only the Ridgeline 2, and the Ridgeline 3 footprint fits only the Ridgeline 3. A footprint that's too large collects rain under the tent.
