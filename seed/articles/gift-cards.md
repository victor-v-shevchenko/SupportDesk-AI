Kestrel gift cards come as e-cards, sent by email, or as physical cards. They can be used on our website for any product.

## Using a gift card

At checkout, enter the 16-digit code and the 4-digit PIN under **Gift card**, then select **Apply**. The amount is taken off your total before you choose another payment method for the rest. If the total doesn't change after you apply the card, don't place the order: refresh the page and apply it again. You can use up to 3 gift cards on one order.

## Balance and expiry

Gift cards don't expire and have no fees. Check the balance under **Gift cards → Check balance**, or on your order confirmation. A gift card can't be exchanged for cash, except where the law requires it.

## Lost cards and problems

We can replace a lost or stolen card if you have the purchase receipt or the original email. If a gift card wasn't applied to an order you already placed, contact us with the order number and the gift card code; we refund the amount that should have been covered and leave it on the gift card.
