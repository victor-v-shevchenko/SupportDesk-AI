---

Your Kestrel account keeps your order history, saved addresses and return labels in one place. You can also order as a guest without an account.

## Resetting your password

Select **Sign in → Forgot password** and enter the email address on your account. The reset email comes from no-reply@kestrel-outfitters.example and is valid for 30 minutes. If it doesn't arrive within 10 minutes, check your spam folder and any filters, and make sure you're using the address you signed up with. Requesting several resets in a row can delay delivery; wait 15 minutes before trying again.

## Locked accounts

After 5 failed sign-in attempts, your account is locked for 30 minutes to protect it. The lock ends by itself; signing in again during the lock restarts the timer. If you need to get in sooner, reset your password, which unlocks the account straight away.

## Changing your email address

Go to **Account → Profile → Email**. We send a confirmation link to the new address, and the change takes effect when you open it. Your order history, saved addresses and gift card balance stay with the account.

## Closing your account

You can ask us to close your account and delete your personal data at any time. Contact support from the email address on the account. We confirm within 2 business days. Records we must keep by law, such as invoices, are kept for the required period and then deleted. Open orders and returns have to be completed first.
