Our support team is here to help with orders, returns, products and your account.

## How to reach us

- **Email:** support@kestrel-outfitters.example, answered within one business day
- **Chat:** on our website, Monday to Friday, 7 am to 6 pm Mountain Time
- **Phone:** 1-800-555-0147, Monday to Friday, 8 am to 5 pm Mountain Time

Kestrel Outfitters is an online store; we don't have shops of our own. A list of independent retailers that stock Kestrel gear is on the **Where to buy** page.

## What to include

To help us answer quickly, include your order number (it starts with KO- and is in your confirmation email), the email address you ordered with, and photos if something is damaged. Never send your full card number, password or security code; we'll never ask for them.

## Safety issues and urgent problems

If a product could be unsafe, stop using it and put "Safety" in the subject line, or call us. Safety reports and orders that have been in transit for more than 14 days go to the front of the queue.

## Feedback

We read all product feedback and pass it to our design team. Send it by email with "Feedback" in the subject line.
