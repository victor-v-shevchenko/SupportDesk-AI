title: Damaged, faulty or wrong items
---

If something arrives damaged, faulty or isn't what you ordered, we'll put it right at no cost to you.

## What to do

Tell us within 14 days of delivery. Contact support with your order number, a short description and photos of the item and, if you still have it, the box. Please keep the packaging until we've finished the claim; the carrier may ask for it.

## Replacement or refund

We send a replacement as soon as we've seen the photos, usually the same or the next business day, with expedited shipping at no charge. If the item is out of stock, or you'd rather not wait, we refund it instead. You don't need to send the damaged item back first: we email you a prepaid label if we need it back, otherwise you can recycle it.

## Wrong item or missing parts

If you got the wrong item, size or color, we send the right one and a prepaid label for the wrong one. For missing parts, such as tent poles, stakes or a stuff sack, we send the part on its own.
